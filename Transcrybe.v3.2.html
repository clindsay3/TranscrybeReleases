Transcrybe 3.2

New regional translation targets on macOS 27 include Arabic (Saudi Arabia), Dutch (Belgium), French (Belgium and Switzerland), German (Austria), and Spanish (Chile). Danish and Swedish live subtitles and translation are now available on Macs running macOS 26.4 or later. Choose separate text alignment for spoken and translated subtitles, with improved caption scaling and resizing. Improved reliability when stopping and immediately restarting live captions, including sessions where no audio was received. Improved Settings window stability, initial layout, and launch behavior on macOS 26. Reduced unnecessary menu bar visibility warnings and refined the menu bar icon shown during onboarding.
